Historical imagery (Esri World Imagery Wayback)

Runs entirely in your browser; imagery and metadata are fetched directly from Esri. Selects by the date the photo was captured, not when the Wayback snapshot was published. Command-line version and source: GitHub.

Area of interest

West/South/East/North describe the box before rotation; rotation turns it about its centre. On the map: drag ↻ to rotate (Shift snaps to 5°), corners to resize, the body to move. The output is rotated so the box is upright, with the ↻ end on the right and a north arrow.

Advanced

Map: the rectangle tool draws the bounding box, which can then be rotated, resized and moved. With "Road segment" selected, click twice to set start and end. "List capture dates" scans all releases at the area center and shows each distinct photo once.
